The key is `canonical_url`, except on `*.myworkdayjobs.com` hosts, where one leading locale segment (`/en-US/`) is dropped and the path is lowercased: Workday serves one posting under each of those spellings, and feeds disagree on them (#132). - `same_company`: every record has the same `normalize(company)`. `company_prefix`: the names differ, but in every pair one equals the other or is the other followed by more words (`aqr` / `aqr capital management`). `different_company`: anything else, such as a parent and a subsidiary or a spelling `normalize` does not join.
- Cross-source: the records' `sources[]` together name two or more sources.
- `Apply URL` is the `canonical_url` of the records' `apply_url`, on a group's first row. When the records spell it differently (a Workday locale segment or path case), every row shows its own record's `canonical_url` instead; `Option 1` and `Sim` still fill only a group's first row.
- Option 1: a group counts when at least one pair of its records has a same or prefix company and a title similarity of 0.6 or more, measured as `is_fuzzy_match` does (`SequenceMatcher` on `normalize`d titles, lower ratio of the two directions). The job-family token clause is not counted; it could add at most the groups in the last row. Job type, language tokens, the term, year and degree-level guard (#123) and location, which `is_fuzzy_match` also checks, are not part of the rule.
- `Sim` lists the title similarity of each same or prefix company pair in the group; `-` when there is none.
